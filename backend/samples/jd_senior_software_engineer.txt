Senior Software Engineer — Platform Infrastructure

About the Role
We're looking for a Senior Software Engineer to join our Platform Infrastructure team. You'll design, build, and maintain scalable backend systems that power our core product used by 2M+ users daily. You'll collaborate closely with product, design, and data teams.

Requirements (Must Have)
- 5+ years of professional software engineering experience
- Strong proficiency in Python and/or Go
- Extensive experience with AWS (EC2, S3, Lambda, RDS, ECS)
- PostgreSQL or MySQL — schema design, query optimization, indexing
- Experience with Docker and Kubernetes for containerization and orchestration
- REST API design and GraphQL experience
- Understanding of CI/CD pipelines (GitHub Actions, Jenkins)
- Strong experience with distributed systems and microservices architecture
- Experience with Redis for caching and session management
- Excellent problem-solving skills and ability to lead technical projects

Nice to Have
- Experience with Kafka or other message queue systems
- Terraform or infrastructure-as-code experience
- Open-source contributions
- Experience with Datadog, Prometheus, or similar observability tools
- React or TypeScript for occasional full-stack work

Responsibilities
- Architect and implement high-throughput, fault-tolerant backend services
- Lead technical design reviews and mentor junior engineers
- Participate in on-call rotation for production systems
- Drive infrastructure cost optimization initiatives
- Write clean, tested, well-documented code

About You
- You take ownership end-to-end, from design to deployment to monitoring
- You communicate clearly in writing and in meetings
- You're comfortable with ambiguity and rapid iteration
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or equivalent experience

We are an equal opportunity employer. Fairness and inclusion are core values.
